Multimodal GenAI Architecture

Behind DermaAI Multimodal Generative AI Screening

A comprehensive look at our three-tier pipeline combining deep-learning vision, ChromaDB vector retrieval, and a local generative AI model to turn a single photo into a grounded, plain-language explanation.

01

Deep Feature Extraction

Uploaded RGB skin lesion photos are normalized, resized to 224×224, and passed through a deep CNN to extract visual characteristics and evaluate class scores.

TensorFlow / Keras CNN Vision
02

Vector RAG Retrieval

Using nomic-embed-text, verified clinical references are chunked into dense embeddings indexed in ChromaDB for factual grounding.

ChromaDB Vector Retrieval
03

Generative AI (GenAI) Synthesis

A localized GenAI language model (llama3.2:3b) synthesizes the retrieved medical facts into plain-language explanations and general precautions.

Local GenAI / Ollama Backend

Grounded, not guessed

Explanations are built from retrieved clinical references, not freeform model memory.

Runs locally

Vision, retrieval, and generation all run on-device via Ollama — no image leaves your machine.

Educational by design

Built to demonstrate a screening pipeline, not to replace clinical diagnosis.

Responsible Generative AI & Clinical Disclaimers

DermaAI is intended strictly for educational research, algorithmic benchmarking, and preliminary health information. An artificial intelligence system or generative language model cannot replicate a biopsy, dermoscopic exam, or clinical staging performed by an accredited dermatologist. Never delay professional medical consultation, disregard physician guidance, or commence/discontinue prescription therapies based on predictions or GenAI explanations rendered by this software.
